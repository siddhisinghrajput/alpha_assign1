Frequently Asked Questions

How long does dispatch & shipping take?

Ready-to-ship objects are dispatched in 1–2 business days. Custom pipe cleaner bouquets and handmade clay pieces take 3–5 days to craft before dispatch. Delivery takes 3–5 business days nationwide.

How are delicate objects packaged?

Each bouquet, ceramic dish, or lamp is wrapped in eco-conscious shock-absorbent craft tissue, honeycomb padding, and sturdy cardboard boxes to guarantee safe arrival.

How do I care for my handmade objects?

Pipe cleaner bouquets never require water! Gently dust them with a soft dry brush. Ceramics and clay vases should be wiped with a damp cloth or soft sponge.

What is your Return & Exchange policy?

Because each piece is handmade in small batches, we accept returns or replacements within 7 days of delivery for damaged or defective items. Custom orders are final sale once approved.

How can I track my order?

Once your order is handed over to our delivery partner, you will receive an SMS and Email with your tracking link and live updates.
